Drift Science

Drift Science is a unified framework for understanding how systems, identities and AI models change over time. This repository contains the core text and concepts from the Drift Science monograph, including The Universal Drift Framework.

Contents

• Monograph — the full theoretical text; includes The Universal Drift Framework (same document)
• Papers — standalone theoretical expansions
• Guess — the introductory paper explaining the basic mechanism of AI drift
• Structural‑Drift — the next step up from Guess; formalised and applied drift mechanics
• Internet‑Proto‑AI‑Ecosystem — analysis of the modern internet as an early multi‑agent AI substrate, showing algorithmic drift, content drift, behavioural drift, and proto‑cascades
• Socio‑Technical‑Cascade — the disaster‑scenario paper: how tiny drift and emergent multi‑AI behaviour create systemic risk and the non‑zero probability of global AI stagnation
• Digital‑Perfection — socio‑behavioural model of internet dating, curated identity, and digital intimacy drift


Keywords

AI drift, system change, identity drift, stability loss, drift dynamics
